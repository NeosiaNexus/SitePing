---
title: What is SitePing?
description: Open-source client feedback widget — DOM-anchored annotations, self-hosted, zero SaaS fees.
---

SitePing lets your clients leave feedback directly on the website you are building for them. They draw a rectangle on the page, type a comment, and you receive it pinned to the exact element — no screenshots pasted in emails, no "the button at the top, no, the other one".
